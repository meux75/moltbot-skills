# PSE Stock Screening Framework

## Overview
A comprehensive screening approach for Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE) stocks, combining technical analysis, fundamental analysis, and risk management.

## Screening Categories

### 1. **Large Cap & Liquid Stocks (PSEi Components)**
**Criteria:**
- Market cap > PHP 50 billion
- Average daily volume > PHP 100M
- PSEi member
- Established track record (>5 years)

**Typical Candidates:**
- BDO (PSE: BDO) - Banking
- SM (PSE: SM) - Diversified conglomerate
- Ayala Corp (PSE: AC) - Diversified conglomerate
- Jollibee Foods (PSE: JFC) - Food & beverage
- PLDT (PSE: TEL) - Telecommunications
- Metrobank (PSE: MBT) - Banking

### 2. **Mid-Cap Growth Candidates**
**Criteria:**
- Market cap PHP 10-50 billion
- Revenue growth > 10% YoY
- Profit margins improving or stable
- Low debt-to-equity (< 1.5x)

**Focus Areas:**
- Technology companies
- Consumer discretionary
- Healthcare services
- Renewable energy

### 3. **Sector Rotation Strategy**
**Rotation Priority by Market Conditions:**

**Bull Market:**
- Consumer discretionary (JFC, SM)
- Technology (TEL, FGEN)
- Real estate (AREIT, Ayala Land)

**Bear Market/Recession:**
- Utilities (MER, MEG)
- Consumer staples (PGI, URC)
- Healthcare (MWC, PSE)

**Recovery Phase:**
- Banking (BDO, MBT)
- Infrastructure (AP, MPI)
- Industrial stocks (DMC, PSE)

### 4. **Technical Screening Criteria**

**Trend-Following Setup:**
- Price above 50-day EMA
- MACD histogram positive
- RSI between 40-70 (not overbought)
- Volume > 1.5x 20-day average
- Relative strength vs PSEi

**Momentum Screens:**
- Price > 200-day moving average
- ROC (Rate of Change) 20-day > 5%
- Support above key levels (52-week high)
- Breakout patterns (cup and handle, flags)

**Value Screens:**
- P/E ratio < sector average
- P/B ratio < 1.5x
- Dividend yield > 2%
- EV/EBITDA < 12x

### 5. **Fundamental Screening**

**Financial Health:**
- Current ratio > 1.5x
- Quick ratio > 1.0x
- Debt-to-equity < 2.0x
- ROE > 10% (consistently)

**Profitability:**
- Gross margin expanding or stable
- Net margin > 8%
- Operating cash flow positive
- FCF yield > 5%

**Growth Metrics:**
- Revenue CAGR > 15% (3-year)
- EPS growth > 12% (3-year)
- Market share gains
- Competitive advantages

### 6. **Risk Assessment**

**Volatility Filters:**
- Beta < 1.5x (vs PSEi)
- ATR 20-day < 5% of price
- No recent earnings misses > 10%
- No regulatory concerns

**Liquidity Check:**
- Daily volume > PHP 50M
- Bid-ask spread < 2%
- No large insider selling
- Covered by analysts

### 7. **Market Timing Indicators**

**Leading Indicators:**
- OFW remittance trends
- Peso strength/weakness
- US Fed policy direction
- Commodity prices (oil, gold)

**Sentiment Indicators:**
- Put/Call ratio
- Insider buying activity
- Analyst ratings upgrades
- Institutional flows

### 8. **Screening Process**

**Weekly Review:**
1. Run technical screens on all PSE stocks
2. Filter for fundamental criteria
3. Check sector rotation signals
4. Assess risk metrics
5. Select 5-10 candidates for deeper analysis

**Daily Watchlist:**
- Track pre-market levels
- Monitor volume anomalies
- News flow and catalysts
- Technical level breaches

**Entry Criteria:**
- 3 of 4 technical criteria met
- Fundamental strength confirmed
- Sector momentum aligns
- Risk/reward ratio > 1:2

### 9. **Portfolio Construction**

**Allocation Strategy:**
- 60% Large caps (stable foundation)
- 30% Mid caps (growth potential)
- 10% Small caps (speculative opportunities)

**Sector Limits:**
- Any single sector < 25% of portfolio
- Financial sector < 35% total
- Tech sector < 20% total

### 10. **Monitoring & Exit Rules**

**Monthly Review:**
- Re-evaluate fundamentals
- Check sector rotation signals
- Assess technical trends
- Rebalance portfolio

**Exit Triggers:**
- Technical breach (stop-loss at 8-12%)
- Fundamental deterioration
- Sector rotation signal
- Profit target (15-35%)

**Macroeconomic:**
- GDP growth rate (target 6-7%)
- Inflation rate (target 2-4%)
- BSP interest rates
- Peso exchange rate
- OFW remittances

**Sector-Specific:**
- Real estate construction permits
